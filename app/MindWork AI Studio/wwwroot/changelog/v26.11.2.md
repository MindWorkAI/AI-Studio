# v26.11.2, build 261 (2026-11-xx xx:xx UTC)
- Added the BeyondFacts Lab as a prototype. It helps researchers prepare step by step for a challenging appearance, such as a panel discussion, an interview, or a hearing. An introduction explains how to work with the lab, and a chat tells you whether the lab fits your task. Each event gets a preparation of its own, which leads you through seven steps, from the details of the event to what you take away, and shows you which key details are still missing. In each step, a chat guides you with questions, based on your details and a guide on science in public debates. You can add material which every step takes into account, such as papers, the recording of an interview, or a poster. Your preparations are part of your data backups. To try it, open the app settings, allow preview features down to prototype, and enable the BeyondFacts Lab. Thanks to Lara Wüster from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) for the concept and the content of the lab.
- Added documents to the Read Web Page tool. The AI can now read PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and OpenDocument files, for example the forms and guidelines in your intranet or the attachments in your Confluence wiki. By default, it reads documents only from your Confluence wiki and from the private hosts you allowed for the tool, and only with a provider you trust highly. You can change this in the settings of the Read Web Page tool.
- Added a size limit for documents which the Read Web Page tool downloads. You find it in the settings of the tool. AI Studio keeps a document only while it reads the text and deletes it right after.
- Added organization-wide management for documents in the Read Web Page tool. IT departments can decide from where the AI may read documents and how large they may be, either fixed or as a default which users may change.
- Added organization-wide management for the results which the Search Confluence tool reads right away. IT departments can decide how many results it reads and how much of their content the AI gets, either fixed or as a default which users may change.
- Improved the Read Web Page tool for pages which show a document in an embedded viewer, such as a PDF on an intranet page. The AI now learns of the document and can open it, where it used to see nothing at all.
- Improved the Search Confluence tool, which now points the AI to the attachments in your wiki, such as PDFs and presentations, so it reads them when they hold the answer.
- Improved the Search Confluence tool, which now reads the best results right away, wiki pages and attachments alike. The AI therefore needs fewer steps, and a question which needs several searches no longer runs out of tool calls before you get an answer. You decide in the settings of the tool how many results it reads.
- Changed the Read Web Page tool to return up to 80,000 characters of a page or document by default, instead of 30,000, so the AI sees more of long pages and documents. If you set a limit of your own in the settings of the tool, it stays as it is.
