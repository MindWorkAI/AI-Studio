# v26.11.2, build 261 (2026-11-xx xx:xx UTC)
- Added the BeyondFacts Lab as a prototype. It helps researchers prepare step by step for a challenging appearance, such as a panel discussion, an interview, or a hearing. Each event gets a preparation of its own, which leads you through seven steps, from the details of the event to what you take away, and shows you which key details are still missing. In each step, a chat guides you with questions, based on your details and a guide on science in public debates. Your preparations are part of your data backups. To try it, open the app settings, allow preview features down to prototype, and enable the BeyondFacts Lab. Thanks to Lara Wüster from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) for the concept and the content of the lab.
- Added the translation of whole documents to the Translation assistant. Choose "Translate document", select a file, and the assistant writes a translated copy of the same type, in which the layout, pictures, tables, and formatting of the original stay as they were. When the translation is done, a dialog asks where to save the copy; your original document stays unchanged.
- Added a choice at the start of the Translation assistant: it asks whether you want to translate a document or a text, and then shows only the fields you need for it.
- Added an option to the settings of the Translation assistant with which you choose how it starts: it asks you every time, always starts with a document, or always starts with a text.
- Added a button next to the gear icon of the Translation assistant with which you switch between translating a document and translating a text at any time.
- Added Word documents (`.docx`) to the document translation of the Translation assistant. It translates the text of the body, tables, text boxes, headers, footers, footnotes, and endnotes, and keeps bold or italic words and links within a sentence. Charts, SmartArt, comments, and the alternative texts of pictures stay as they are.
- Added PowerPoint presentations (`.pptx`) to the document translation of the Translation assistant. It translates the text on all slides, including groups and tables, and in the speaker notes, and keeps bold or italic words and links within a sentence. Charts, SmartArt, and the masters and layouts of the slides stay as they are.
- Added Markdown files (`.md`) to the document translation of the Translation assistant. Code, formulas, link targets, and embedded HTML stay as they are.
- Added web pages (`.html`, `.htm`) to the document translation of the Translation assistant. It translates the text of a page as well as its title, its description, the alternative texts of pictures, and tooltips, and marks the page with its new language. Scripts, styles, and code stay as they are.
- Added LaTeX documents (`.tex`) to the document translation of the Translation assistant. It translates paragraphs, headings, captions, lists, tables, the title, and the slides of Beamer presentations. Formulas, code, drawings, and comments stay as they are, and so do the language settings of packages such as babel.
- Added a summary to the document translation of the Translation assistant, which tells you how many sections of your document it translated and which parts of a document of this kind stay as they are. Sections in which the security check finds suspicious content stay untranslated, and the summary tells you how many there are.
- Added a warning to the document translation of the Translation assistant for sections in which the AI model did not keep all formatting, such as a link or a bold word. The translated words of such a section are still in your document, only parts of its formatting are missing.
