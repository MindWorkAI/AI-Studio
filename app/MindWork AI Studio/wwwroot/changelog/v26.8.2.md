# v26.8.2, build 255 (2026-08-xx xx:xx UTC)
- Added protection against prompt injection. Documents, web pages, and retrieved content can carry instructions written for the AI rather than for you, for example, text telling it to ignore its rules or to hand over its instructions. AI Studio now removes such passages before the content reaches a model, while the rest of your document stays intact and usable. When something was removed, AI Studio tells you and can show you which passages it took out. You can turn the protection off in the chat settings. For IT departments: the new settings `DataChat.EnablePromptInjectionProtection` and `DataChat.ShowPromptInjectionAlert` let you configure this for your organization.
